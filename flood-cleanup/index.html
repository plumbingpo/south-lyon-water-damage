Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. Floodwater is almost always rated Category 2 (gray water) or Category 3 (black water), so it carries bacteria, contaminants and possibly hazardous material that can make people seriously ill. South Lyon Restoration handles emergency flood cleanup across South Lyon and answers 24/7 to pump out the water, sanitize what it touched and restore your property safely.

Cleanup opens with rapid extraction: truck-mounted units and submersible pumps that pull thousands of gallons an hour out of the building. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. Anything unsalvageable comes out and is hauled off correctly, including wet insulation, drywall that took in contaminated water and waterlogged carpet padding.

Once the space is extracted and sanitized, our team brings in commercial drying gear, namely low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers plus high-velocity air movers, so the structural components dry all the way through. We check moisture levels every day with calibrated meters and thermal imaging so the drying is complete and mold does not get a start. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

What a Professional Brings to Flood Cleanup

Flood cleanup is far more than pumping out standing water. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. A trained cleanup team knows how to set containment, wear PPE (personal protective equipment) correctly and sanitize by protocol, which protects your household from hepatitis, E. coli and other diseases carried in water.

Beyond the health concerns, a poor flood cleanup often leaves hidden moisture that turns into severe mold growth within days. Professional technicians use moisture meters, thermal imaging and psychrometric calculations to make sure every affected area, including wall cavities, subfloors and framing, reaches an acceptable drying standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.

Flood Cleanup Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Add downspout extensions so roof water empties well away from the foundation
  • Add a sump pump with battery backup, since power outages and storms tend to arrive together
  • Wherever flooding is likely, get appliances and electrical equipment up above floor level
  • Check that grading on all sides of the house carries water away from the foundation
  • Look into a flood insurance policy, including when your home sits outside a mapped flood zone
  • Cracks in the foundation should be sealed and the basement walls given a waterproof coating

Signs It Is Time for Flood Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water coming in through foundation cracks, window wells or door thresholds
  • Stains along baseboards and lower walls that mark the flood level
  • Heavy rainfall, a dead sump pump and water spreading across the basement
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through
  • A sewer or drain backup pushing contaminated water into living spaces
  • Water collecting along the foundation because the yard is saturated
  • Soft, wet drywall or carpeting that is soaked through
