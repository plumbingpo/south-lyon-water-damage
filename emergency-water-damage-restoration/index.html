Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Find your main water shutoff today and make sure everyone in the house can close it.
  • Any trip beyond a few days is a reason to turn the main supply off before you go.
  • Save an emergency restoration company's number in your contacts ahead of time.
  • Store valuables and important papers off the floor, especially in basements and lower levels.
  • Put leak alarms beneath sinks and beside the dishwasher, washer and water heater.
  • Being able to find the electrical panel quickly means wet areas can be de-energized without risk.

Warning Signs for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
  • Walls on the lower floor are wet from top to bottom because of a leak overhead.
  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.
  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • Water has reached the electrical panel, appliances or power strips on the floor.
  • The house was flooded when you walked in, with no telling how many hours it had been going.
