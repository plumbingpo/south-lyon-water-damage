Selecting a Restoration Professional in South Lyon, MI

Home / Blog / Choosing a Water Damage Restoration Company in South Lyon, MI

After water damage at a South Lyon, MI property, few choices matter more than which restoration company you hire. That choice affects the speed of the restoration, how completely the damage gets fixed, what comes out of your own pocket, and whether mold or other secondary problems appear months down the road. Unfortunately, the restoration industry has its share of unqualified operators who may leave you with incomplete work, inflated bills, or worse. Here is what to look for when sorting the best restoration firms from everyone else.

Check for IICRC Certification

The Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification (IICRC) is the gold standard for restoration industry credentials. To earn certification, a technician trains rigorously in restoration science, covering mold prevention, moisture measurement, structural drying, and proper extraction technique. Look for two current IICRC certifications on the crew: WRT (Water Damage Restoration) and ASD (Applied Structural Drying). Companies that cannot verify these credentials should be avoided.

Confirm Insurance and Licensing

Any company you let work in your home needs full insurance, meaning general liability as well as workers' compensation. Ask to see the insurance certificates, then check that they have not expired. Without proper coverage, you could be liable for injuries or additional property damage that occur during the restoration. Licensing matters too, so confirm the firm holds whatever business licenses South Lyon or Michigan requires.

Check Availability and Arrival Speed

Water damage is a time-sensitive emergency. Costs and damage climb with every hour that passes. The restoration company you choose should offer true 24/7 emergency response with fast arrival times in your area. Ask specifically about their response time to South Lyon and confirm that their emergency line is answered by a live person, not a voicemail or answering service that adds delay.

Look at Their Equipment and Drying Process

Good restoration work depends on professional-grade equipment. Find out what they extract water with (truck-mounted units beat portable-only setups), which dehumidifiers they run (the industry standard is LGR), and if thermal imaging is used to find moisture. Also ask about their drying monitoring process: reputable companies monitor moisture levels daily and adjust equipment based on readings, not just place equipment and hope for the best.

Read Reviews and Call References

A company's history shows up clearly in its online reviews. Search Yelp, Google, and the BBB to see if customer satisfaction, professional conduct, and work quality hold up consistently. Pay particular attention to reviews that mention the company's communication, timeliness, insurance assistance, and the final quality of restoration work. It is also fair to ask for the names of recent South Lyon water damage customers you can contact.

Know How They Price and Bill Insurance

A reputable company hands you a detailed estimate before any work starts and can clearly explain how its prices are set. Many quality companies use industry-standard pricing software (like Xactimate) that insurance companies recognize and accept. Ask whether the company works directly with insurance carriers and whether they can bill your insurance directly. Companies that demand large upfront payments or refuse to work with insurance should raise concerns.
