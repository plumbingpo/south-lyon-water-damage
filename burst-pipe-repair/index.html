Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

South Lyon Restoration provides 24/7 emergency response for burst pipe water damage throughout South Lyon and surrounding areas. As soon as the flow is shut down, extraction starts: portable and truck-mounted units pull standing water out of every affected area as fast as we can.

Structural drying comes next and covers the wet drywall, the framing, the insulation, the subfloor and the finished flooring above it. Burst pipes often damage multiple rooms and multiple levels, because water follows gravity through wall cavities, floor assemblies and ceiling spaces. What a Professional Brings to Burst Pipe Repair

Burst pipe water damage often extends far beyond what is visible on the surface. Water travels through wall cavities, along electrical conduits, through floor assemblies and between ceiling joists, saturating materials in places you may not discover for days or weeks. At that point mold can already be growing out of sight, and the structure may have lost a significant amount of strength. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

With only 24 to 48 hours before mold starts, you need equipment this fast and this strong.

Warning Signs for Burst Pipe Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • The sound of running water when no fixtures are in use
  • Frost or ice on exposed pipes during cold weather
  • Stale or earthy smells coming from a cabinet or wall cavity
  • Ceilings or walls that show damp patches, stains or a swollen bulge
  • A sudden drop in water pressure throughout your property
  • Puddles on the floor that you cannot trace to anything obvious
  • Tap water that turns brown, yellow or cloudy

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • During freezing weather, keep faucets dripping and cabinet doors open so heat can circulate
  • Before winter, drain outside spigots and take the hoses off them
  • Leave the heat on when you travel in winter so indoor pipes stay above freezing
  • Replace old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes before they fail unexpectedly
  • Every few years, ask a licensed plumber to look over the system for corrosion and worn parts
  • Monitor your water pressure, because excessive pressure (above 80 PSI) strains pipes and fittings
